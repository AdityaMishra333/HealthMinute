HealthMinute

An emergency response platform that shortens the gap between an accident and medical help. A user reports an accident with their location, and hospitals and ambulance drivers see it instantly and can respond.

Why

Most emergency response systems rely on a phone call and manual coordination. HealthMinute puts hospitals, drivers, and reporters on the same real-time feed, so the time from "accident happens" to "help is moving" is as short as possible.

How it works
A user opens the app and reports an accident with one tap; their location is captured automatically.
The report appears instantly on hospital and ambulance driver dashboards.
A hospital can accept the alert to confirm they're aware and responding.
A driver can accept the case and get one-tap navigation to the accident site via Google Maps.
